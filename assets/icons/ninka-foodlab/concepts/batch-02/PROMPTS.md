## Recipe version

```text
Use case: logo-brand icon-system exploration.
Asset type: Ninka FoodLab desktop app icon concept sheet for the business object “配方版本 / Recipe Version”.
Reference image: use the attached mixed-icon pressure-test board as the strict visual-language reference. Match its approved custom icons, not the surrounding UI layout.
Primary request: design one production-minded recipe-version icon direction. The symbol should communicate an immutable saved recipe snapshot, clearly distinct from the existing ingredient-version icon. Use a compact rounded recipe sheet or card with two short formula lines and one simple version/snapshot cue, such as a small sealed tab or restrained circular history mark. Keep the silhouette simple enough to remain readable at 16px.
Style/medium: clean flat vector icon concept, 24×24-grid logic, visually equivalent to 1.75px rounded outline, round caps and joins, low center of gravity, generous negative space.
Composition: cream square presentation board; one large centered icon; three small scale previews aligned below representing 16px, 20px, and 24px. No device UI, no mock application screen.
Color palette: Forest #153D36 primary, Grain #EFBD50 used only once as a restrained accent, Cream #FFF7E7 background.
Constraints: one coherent icon only; no shadows, gradients, texture, 3D, photorealism, letters, numbers, captions, watermark, or decorative frame. Avoid overly dense stacked pages, thin hairlines, generic database cylinders, and four extra seed marks. The large icon and all small previews must depict exactly the same geometry.
```
